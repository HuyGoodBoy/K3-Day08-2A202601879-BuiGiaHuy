# Library Services and Study Spaces at RMIT

The RMIT Library provides comprehensive resources and study spaces to support student learning.

## Library Hours

### Saigon South Campus
- Monday to Friday: 7:30 AM - 10:00 PM
- Saturday: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- **Exam period**: Extended to midnight

### Hanoi Campus
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM

## Study Spaces

### Silent Study Zone (Level 3)
- Individual study desks
- No mobile phone use
- Absolute silence required

### Group Study Rooms
- Available on Levels 2 and 4
- **Booking required**: Via library portal
- Maximum 6 students per room
- Whiteboard and screen available
- **2-hour limit** per booking

### Collaborative Learning Area (Level 1)
- Open discussion area
- Group work permitted
- Light refreshments allowed

## Digital Resources

Access thousands of e-books, academic journals, and databases:
- Scopus, Web of Science
- IEEE Xplore
- ProQuest
- JSTOR

## Services Offered

- Research consultation appointments
- APA/Harvard referencing workshops
- EndNote and Zotero training
- Inter-library loan (ILL)
