# Campus Facilities and Recreation at RMIT Vietnam

RMIT Vietnam campuses offer world-class facilities for learning, recreation, and community building.

## Sports and Recreation

### Fitness Centre (Gym)
- **Location**: Level 2, Building 2, Saigon South Campus
- **Hours**: 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM daily
- **Equipment**: Modern gym equipment, free weights, cardio machines
- **Access**: Student ID card required
- **Fee**: Included in student services fee

### Sports Courts
- Basketball court (outdoor)
- Tennis courts (2 courts)
- Multi-purpose court (volleyball, badminton)
- **Booking**: Via Sports Office or online portal

### Swimming Pool
- Outdoor pool at Saigon South Campus
- Open: 6:00 AM - 8:00 PM
- Free for all students
- Swimming lessons available (extra fee)

## Student Spaces

### Student Lounge
- Level 1, Building 1
- Comfortable seating areas
- Vending machines and café
- Study tables available

### Student Hub
- Central meeting place
- Event space for student activities
- Lockers available for rent
- Post office services

### Food and Dining

Multiple dining options across campus:
- Main Cafeteria (Building 1): Vietnamese and international cuisine
- Coffee shops: Starbucks, Highlands Coffee
- Food court (Level 1): Various Asian cuisines
- **Opening hours**: 7:00 AM - 9:00 PM

## IT and Technology

### Computer Labs
- 8 computer labs across campuses
- Software: Microsoft Office, Adobe Creative Suite, programming tools
- **Printing**: Prepaid card system available
- Free Wi-Fi throughout campus

### Makerspace
- 3D printers
- Laser cutting
- Electronics workshop
- Book time slots via online portal
