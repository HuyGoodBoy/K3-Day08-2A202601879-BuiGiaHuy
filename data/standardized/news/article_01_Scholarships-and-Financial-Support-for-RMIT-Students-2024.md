# Scholarships and Financial Support for RMIT Students 2024

RMIT University Vietnam offers a wide range of scholarships to support students throughout their academic journey.

## Available Scholarships

### Academic Excellence Scholarship
For students who have achieved outstanding academic results in their previous studies.
- **Value**: Up to 50% tuition fee waiver
- **Eligibility**: GPA 8.5/10 or equivalent
- **Duration**: Renewable each semester

### Financial Hardship Scholarship
Designed for students facing genuine financial difficulties.
- **Value**: Up to 70% tuition fee waiver
- **Eligibility**: Income assessment required
- **Documents**: Family income certificate, hardship statement

### Sports and Arts Excellence Scholarship
Recognises outstanding achievements in sports, arts, and extracurricular activities.
- **Value**: 10-30 million VND per award
- **Eligibility**: National/international competition records required

## Application Process

1. Submit online application via Student Portal
2. Upload required documents
3. Attend scholarship interview (if shortlisted)
4. Results announced via email within 4 weeks

## Key Dates for 2024
- **Application opens**: 1 June 2024
- **Application deadline**: 31 July 2024
- **Results announcement**: By 15 August 2024
