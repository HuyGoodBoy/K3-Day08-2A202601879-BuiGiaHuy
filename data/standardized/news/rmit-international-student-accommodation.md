# Accommodation for international students

The cost of living in Vietnam can vary depending on your lifestyle and location within the country.

While you’re studying, it is important to have a home base where you feel comfortable and secure. Here's a rough estimate of monthly expenses for international students in Hanoi and HCMC.

Choosing the right place to live is an important part of your journey at RMIT Vietnam. Before signing any lease or paying money, take time to explore your options and consider what works best for your budget, lifestyle, and preferred location.

***Note: RMIT Vietnam do not have on-campus housing. However, we provide a range of support services and resources to help you find safe and suitable accommodation near campus.

## How can we support

- Accommodation recommendation listings: Access our curated database of rental properties, including shared houses, apartments, and studio options near RMIT campuses.

- Tenant advice: Get guidance on understanding rental contracts, your rights and responsibilities, and how to avoid common rental issues in Vietnam.

## Rental advice

Most international students choose to live in shared or private rentals in the surrounding areas. Here's what you need to know:

- Rental market in Vietnam: Most properties are basic furnished, and utilities (electricity, water, internet or management fee) are might not include in the rent. Be sure to check what's included before signing a lease.

- Upfront costs: Be prepared for additional expenses such as (security deposit), the first month’s rent.

- Lease agreements: Leases are commonly 6-12 months, but shorter or more flexible terms may be negotiable directly with landlords or agents.

- Refer to Accommodation Advice for International Students PDF guide to know your rights & responsibilities.
