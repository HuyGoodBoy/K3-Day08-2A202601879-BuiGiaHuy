# Enrolment

### Discover how to enrol as a new or continuing student, as well as apply for credit or leave of absence.

Students are not required to be on campus to enrol; they just need to access the Enrolment tile in myRMIT to do so.

Students can log in to myRMIT using either their Student ID (S-numbers) or RMIT email address (s-numbers@rmit.edu.vn).

Step-by-step process for enrolling as a new student at RMIT Vietnam.

## Manage your enrolment

### Apply for credit

If you have completed relevant study or have relevant work or life experiences in the area of your current study at RMIT, you should consider applying for credit.
