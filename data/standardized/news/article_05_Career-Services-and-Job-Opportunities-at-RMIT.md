# Career Services and Job Opportunities at RMIT

RMIT's Career Hub connects students with industry opportunities and professional development.

## Career Hub Services

### Career Counselling
- One-on-one career consultations
- Career aptitude testing
- Industry exposure sessions
- **Book**: Via Career Hub online portal

### Resume and Interview Preparation
- Resume/CV reviews
- Cover letter workshops
- Mock interview sessions
- LinkedIn profile optimisation

### Job Board
Access exclusive job and internship opportunities:
- Part-time positions
- Internship programs
- Graduate roles
- Industry projects

## Employer Connections

### Career Fairs
- **Annual Career Fair**: February (Saigon South)
- **Industry Connect Days**: Monthly
- Meet recruiters from top employers
- Networking opportunities

### Employer Site Visits
- Company visits throughout the year
- Industry expert talks
- Site tours for select programs

## Internship Program

### Credit-bearing Internship
- Required for most undergraduate programs
- Minimum 10 weeks full-time equivalent
- Supervised by industry mentor + academic supervisor
- Graded on reflection report and employer evaluation

### Non-credit Internship
- Voluntary positions
- Posted on Career Hub job board
- Coordinated by Career Services

## Graduate Employment

RMIT Vietnam graduate employment rate:
- **93% employed within 6 months** of graduation
- Average starting salary: 12-18 million VND/month
- Top employing industries: Technology, Finance, Consulting

## Student Employment

Part-time job opportunities on campus:
- Library assistant
- Research assistant
- IT support
- Student ambassador
- **Pay**: 35,000 - 50,000 VND/hour
