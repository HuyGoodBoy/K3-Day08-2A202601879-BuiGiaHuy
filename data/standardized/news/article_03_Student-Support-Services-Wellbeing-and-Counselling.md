# Student Support Services - Wellbeing and Counselling

RMIT Vietnam is committed to supporting student wellbeing throughout their university experience.

## Health and Wellbeing Services

### On-campus Medical Clinic
- **Location**: Ground floor, Building 1, Saigon South Campus
- **Hours**: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- **Services**: Basic healthcare, first aid, health referrals
- **Emergency**: Call campus security at ext. 111

### Counselling Services
Professional, confidential counselling available for all students.
- **Individual counselling**: Book via online portal
- **Session duration**: 50 minutes
- **Number of sessions**: Up to 8 per semester (free)
- **Issues supported**: Academic stress, anxiety, relationships, cultural adjustment

## Academic Support

### Peer Tutoring Program
- Free tutoring for challenging subjects
- Book via Student Portal
- Available subjects: Mathematics, Physics, Programming, English

### Academic Skills Workshops
- Time management
- Note-taking strategies
- Exam preparation
- Presentation skills

### Writing Centre
- One-on-one writing consultations
- Feedback on assignments
- APA/Harvard referencing help

## Accessibility Services

Support for students with disabilities or learning differences:
- Extended exam time
- Alternative assessment arrangements
- Note-taking assistance
- Accessible facilities

## International Student Support

Dedicated support for international students:
- Visa and immigration advice
- Cultural adaptation programs
- International student buddy system
